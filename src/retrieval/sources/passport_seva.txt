PASSPORT SEVA APPLICATION GUIDELINES
Official Portal: https://www.passportindia.gov.in/
Source Title: Passport Seva - Ministry of External Affairs

Overview:
Passport Seva Project, administered by the Consular, Passport & Visa (CPV) Division of the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), provides citizen-centric passport services across India through Passport Seva Kendras (PSK), Post Office Passport Seva Kendras (POPSK), and official online portals.

Types of Passport Applications:
1. Fresh Passport: Applied for the first time.
2. Re-issue of Passport: Applied when existing passport has expired, pages are exhausted, or details (address/name) are updated.
3. Tatkaal Passport: Urgent processing category for fast-track passport issuance.

Eligibility Criteria:
- Applicant must be an Indian citizen.
- Applicants of any age (including minors and senior citizens) can apply with valid proof.

Documents Required:
1. Proof of Date of Birth: Birth Certificate, Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, School Leaving Certificate, or Driving License.
2. Proof of Present Address: Aadhaar Card, Bank Passbook (with photo), Electricity Bill, Water Bill, Rent Agreement, or Voter ID.
3. Non-ECR Proof (for ECNR status): Class 10th pass certificate or higher educational qualification certificate, income tax assessment order, or proof of age above 50.
4. For Re-issue: Original existing passport and self-attested photocopies of first two and last two pages.

Application Process:
1. Register on official portal (https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) and log in to user account.
2. Click 'Apply for Fresh Passport / Re-issue of Passport'.
3. Fill out online application form (or download e-form, fill offline, and upload).
4. Click 'Pay and Schedule Appointment' to select nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK/POPSK) and choose an available date/time slot.
5. Pay application fee online (Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, or SBI Challan).
6. Print application receipt containing Application Reference Number (ARN).
7. Visit designated PSK/POPSK on appointment date with original documents for biometric capture and document verification.
8. Police Verification is initiated locally before/after passport dispatch depending on application category.

Verification Note:
Fee structures, Tatkaal rules, and appointment availability should be verified directly on the official portal (https://www.passportindia.gov.in/).
