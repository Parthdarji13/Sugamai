INCOME CERTIFICATE APPLICATION GUIDELINES
Official Portal: https://services.india.gov.in/
Source Title: National Government Services Portal - Income Certificate Guidelines

Overview:
An Income Certificate is an official document issued by the state government authority that certifies the annual income of an individual or a family from all sources (salary, agriculture, business, pension, rental, etc.). It is widely used to claim scholarships, fee concessions, government subsidies, and access to other social welfare schemes.

Eligibility Criteria:
- The applicant must be a citizen of India.
- The applicant must be a resident of the state/union territory where they are applying.
- The family's combined income from all sources must be declared honestly.

Documents Required:
1. Proof of Identity: Aadhaar Card, Voter ID Card, PAN Card, or Passport.
2. Proof of Address: Ration Card, Electricity Bill, Water Bill, or Telephone Bill.
3. Proof of Income:
   - For salaried individuals: Form 16, Salary Slip, or Income Tax Return (ITR).
   - For businessmen/professionals: Self-declaration, ITR, or audit reports.
   - For farmers/agriculturists: Land revenue records (e.g., 7/12 extract in some states), Talati/Patwari declaration.
   - For pensioners: Pension payment order (PPO) or bank passbook.
4. Passport-sized photograph.
5. Self-declaration affidavit.

Application Process:
- Online Method:
  1. Visit the official State e-District portal or Citizen Service Portal (e.g., Digital Gujarat, e-District Delhi, MahaOnline, ServicePlus).
  2. Register or log in to the portal.
  3. Search for "Issue of Income Certificate" under revenue services.
  4. Fill in the application form with personal, address, and income details.
  5. Upload the scanned copies of required documents and a photograph.
  6. Pay the nominal processing fee (varies by state, typically Rs. 10 - Rs. 50).
  7. Submit the application and save the acknowledgment/application number for tracking.
- Offline Method:
  1. Visit the local Tahsildar Office, Mamlatdar Office, Revenue Office, or authorized Citizen Service Centre (CSC) / Jan Seva Kendra.
  2. Obtain and fill out the physical application form.
  3. Attach self-attested photocopies of all required documents.
  4. Submit the form to the officer and obtain a receipt.
  5. The verification is typically done by the local Revenue Inspector or Talati.
  6. The certificate is issued within 10 to 15 days upon successful verification.
