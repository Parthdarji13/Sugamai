E-SHRAM CARD - NATIONAL DATABASE OF UNORGANIZED WORKERS (NDUW)
Official Portal: https://eshram.gov.in/
Source Title: e-Shram Portal - Ministry of Labour & Employment

Overview:
e-Shram is a comprehensive national database created by the Ministry of Labour & Employment to register and deliver social security benefits to unorganized workers across India. Registered workers are issued an e-Shram Card containing a unique 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN).

Eligible Worker Categories:
Unorganized workers aged between 16 and 59 years, including:
- Construction workers, plumbers, masons, painters, electricians.
- Small and marginal farmers, agricultural laborers, sharecroppers.
- Street vendors, hawkers, rickshaw pullers, coolies, head-load workers.
- Domestic workers, maids, tailors, weavers, handicraft artisans.
- Gig and platform workers, delivery agents, drivers.

Key Benefits:
1. Universal Account Number (UAN): A portable 12-digit identification valid across all states in India.
2. Accidental Insurance Cover: Rs. 2 Lakh cover in case of accidental death or permanent disability, and Rs. 1 Lakh for partial disability under Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY).
3. Social Security Scheme Integration: Seamless integration for government welfare schemes (such as PMSYM pension scheme, ration benefits, emergency relief funds during disasters).

Eligibility Exclusions:
- Workers who are members of EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund) or ESIC (Employees' State Insurance).
- Individuals who pay Income Tax in the last assessment year.
- Persons under 16 years or above 59 years of age.

Documents Required:
1. Aadhaar Card (with active mobile number linked for OTP verification).
2. Aadhaar-linked Active Bank Account details (Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC Code).
3. Occupation details and educational qualification proof (optional).

Self-Registration Process:
1. Visit the official e-Shram portal (https://eshram.gov.in/) and click 'Register on e-Shram'.
2. Enter Aadhaar-linked mobile number and captcha code, then enter OTP received.
3. Verify Aadhaar details using OTP/Biometric.
4. Fill in personal details, address, occupation, educational qualification, and bank details.
5. Preview and submit the form. Download and print the e-Shram Card instantly.

Verification Note:
Worker registration is free of cost. Check current registration status and update profile details at the official portal (https://eshram.gov.in/).

Scheme Duration, Validity & Last Date:
- Last Date / Application Deadline: There is NO last date or closing deadline for e-Shram registration. Registration remains open 24/7 year-round.
- Card Validity: The e-Shram UWIN card is valid for a lifetime. Workers can update their occupation, address, or bank details at any time.
