PRADHAN MANTRI AWAS YOJANA (PMAY) - HOUSING FOR ALL
Official Portal: https://pmaymis.gov.in/
Source Title: PMAY Portal - Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs

Overview:
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a flagship initiative of the Government of India launched to ensure a pucca house for all eligible urban and rural families. The scheme operates through two verticals: PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U) managed by MoHUA, and PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G) managed by the Ministry of Rural Development.

Key Components & Benefits:
1. Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS): Interest subsidy on home loans for EWS, LIG, and MIG categories buying or constructing their first pucca house.
2. Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC): Direct financial assistance (typically Rs. 1.5 Lakh to Rs. 2.5 Lakh depending on state/central share) to eligible families owning land to construct a new house.
3. Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP): Assistance for affordable housing projects built in partnership with public/private sectors.
4. In-Situ Slum Redevelopment (ISSR): Rehabilitation of slum dwellers using land as a resource.

Eligibility Criteria:
- The beneficiary family must not own a pucca house (an all-weather home) anywhere in India in their name or in the name of any family member.
- Income categories:
  - Economically Weaker Section (EWS): Annual household income up to Rs. 3 Lakh.
  - Low Income Group (LIG): Annual household income between Rs. 3 Lakh and Rs. 6 Lakh.
  - Middle Income Group (MIG-I & MIG-II): Income guidelines specified as per ministry notifications.
- Preference is given to female ownership or co-ownership of the house.

Documents Required:
1. Proof of Identity & Age: Aadhaar Card (mandatory), Voter ID, PAN Card.
2. Proof of Income: Salary slip, Form 16, Income Certificate from Revenue Authority, or Self-declaration affidavit.
3. Property Documents: Land ownership proof (7/12 extract, registry deed), approved building plan, and site photograph.
4. Bank Account Details: Aadhaar-linked bank account passbook.

Application Process:
- For PMAY-Urban:
  1. Visit official PMAY-U portal (https://pmaymis.gov.in/) or visit nearest Common Service Center (CSC).
  2. Navigate to 'Citizen Assessment' and select relevant category (e.g. Beneficiary Led Construction).
  3. Enter Aadhaar number and fill out personal, income, and bank details.
  4. Submit application and download assessment form for tracking.
- For PMAY-Gramin:
  1. Beneficiary selection is based on Housing Deprivation parameters in SECC 2011 data verified by Gram Sabha.

Verification Note:
Specific subsidy amounts, active CLSS guidelines, and beneficiary lists should be verified on the official portals (https://pmaymis.gov.in/ and https://pmayg.nic.in/).

Scheme Duration, Validity & Last Date:
- Last Date / Extension: The Cabinet has extended Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY Urban 2.0 & PMAY Gramin) to cover 3 crore additional houses through 2028-2029.
- Application Deadline: Applications remain open continuously on the official PMAY portal and through local Gram Panchayats / CSC centers.
