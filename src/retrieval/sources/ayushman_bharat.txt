AYUSHMAN BHARAT - PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (AB-PMJAY)
Official Portal: https://pmjay.gov.in/
Source Title: National Health Authority - Ayushman Bharat Portal

Overview:
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the largest health assurance scheme in the world which aims to provide a health cover of Rs. 5 lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization to over 12 crores poor and vulnerable families (approximately 55 crore beneficiaries) that form the bottom 40% of the Indian population.

Benefits:
- Provides cover of up to Rs. 5,00,000 (Five Lakhs) per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
- Provides cashless and paperless access to health care services for the beneficiary at the point of service in any empanelled public or private hospital across India.
- Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days post-hospitalization expenses such as diagnostics, medicines, and consultation.
- No restriction on family size, age, or gender.
- All pre-existing medical conditions are covered from day one.
- Coverage Reset: The ₹5 Lakh health insurance cover resets automatically every financial year for eligible family beneficiaries.

Eligibility Criteria (Rural Families):
Under SECC-2011, rural eligibility is determined based on the following deprivation categories (D1 to D7):
- D1: Only one room with kucha walls and kucha roof.
- D2: No adult member between age 16 to 59.
- D3: Female headed households with no adult male member between age 16 to 59.
- D4: Disabled member and no able-bodied adult member.
- D5: Landless households deriving major part of their income from manual casual labour.
- D7: Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) households.
- Automatically included: Destitute, living on alms, manual scavenger households, primitive tribal groups, legally released bonded labour.

Eligibility Criteria (Urban Families):
Under SECC-2011, urban eligibility is defined based on 11 occupational categories:
1. Ragpicker
2. Beggar
3. Domestic worker
4. Street vendor / Cobbler / hawker / Other service provider working on streets
5. Construction worker / Plumber / Mason / Labor / Painter / Welder / Security guard / Coolie and other head-load worker
6. Sweeper / Sanitation worker / Mali
7. Home-based worker / Artisan / Tailor / Handicraft worker
8. Transport worker / Driver / Conductor / Helper to drivers and conductors / Cart puller / Rickshaw puller
9. Shop worker / Assistant / Peon in small establishment / Helper / Delivery assistant / Attendant / Waiter
10. Washerman / Chowkidar
11. Electrician / Mechanic / Assembler / Repair worker

Senior Citizens (Aged 70+):
- All senior citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible for dedicated health insurance cover of ₹5 Lakh per year under the expanded Ayushman Bharat scheme, regardless of socio-economic status.

Documents Required:
To verify eligibility and generate an Ayushman Card (PM-JAY Golden Card), beneficiaries need:
1. Aadhaar Card: Primary identification document mandatory for biometric / OTP-based e-KYC authentication.
2. Ration Card / Family ID: Proof of family composition and SECC database linkage.
3. Active Mobile Number: Required for receiving OTP during authentication and card download.
4. Government Photo ID (Voter ID / Driving License) in case of auxiliary verification.

How to Apply & How to Get Ayushman Card (Application Process):
Eligible beneficiaries can obtain their Ayushman Card through any of the following methods:
1. Online via Beneficiary Portal (https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/) or Ayushman App:
   - Visit https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/ or open the official Ayushman App.
   - Login with your mobile number and authenticate via OTP.
   - Select your State, Scheme (PMJAY), and search by Aadhaar Number, Family ID, or PMJAY ID.
   - Complete e-KYC using Aadhaar OTP, Fingerprint, or Face Authentication.
   - Download and print the generated Ayushman Card.
2. Offline at Empanelled Hospital (Ayushman Mitra Help Desk):
   - Visit any empanelled public or private hospital.
   - Meet the Ayushman Mitra at the dedicated Ayushman help desk.
   - Provide your Aadhaar Card and Ration Card to verify eligibility and generate the card on the spot.
3. Offline at Common Service Center (CSC / Jan Seva Kendra):
   - Visit the nearest CSC with Aadhaar card and Ration card.
   - The CSC operator will perform e-KYC and issue the Ayushman Card.

Scheme Duration, Validity & Last Date:
- Last Date / Application Deadline: There is NO last date or closing deadline for Ayushman Bharat (AB-PMJAY). It is a continuous, permanent national health scheme of the Government of India.
- Scheme Validity & Registration: Eligible citizens and senior citizens (aged 70+) can apply for and generate their Ayushman Card at any time throughout the year.
- Benefit Portability: The Ayushman card is portable across all empanelled hospitals in India.
