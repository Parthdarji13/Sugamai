PRADHAN MANTRI MUDRA YOJANA (PMMY) - BUSINESS LOAN GUIDELINES
Official Portal: https://www.mudra.org.in/
Source Title: Mudra Portal - Ministry of Finance

Overview:
Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) is a scheme initiated by the Government of India to provide collateral-free business loans up to Rs. 10 Lakh to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises (MSMEs). Loans are disbursed through Commercial Banks, RRBs, Small Finance Banks, MFIs, and NBFCs.

Mudra Loan Categories:
1. Shishu: Loans up to Rs. 50,000/- (aimed at startups and new small entrepreneurs).
2. Kishor: Loans above Rs. 50,000/- and up to Rs. 5 Lakh (for existing micro enterprises expanding operations).
3. Tarun: Loans above Rs. 5 Lakh and up to Rs. 10 Lakh (for established business units upgrading machinery or infrastructure).

Eligibility Criteria:
- Any Indian citizen who has a viable business plan for a non-farm sector income-generating activity (manufacturing, trading, services, agriculture-allied activities like poultry/dairy).
- The applicant must not be a defaulter to any bank or financial institution.

Key Benefits:
- Collateral-Free Credit: No collateral security required for loans up to Rs. 10 Lakh under Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU).
- Flexible Repayment: Repayment tenure typically ranges between 3 and 5 years.
- Mudra Card: Issued as a RuPay debit card for convenient working capital drawdown.

Documents Required:
1. Proof of Identity: Voter ID, Aadhaar Card, Driving License, or PAN Card.
2. Proof of Residence: Utility bill, Bank passbook, or Aadhaar Card.
3. Business Proof: Business registration certificate, GST registration, trade license, or partnership deed.
4. Financial Proof: Bank account statements for past 6 months, quotation for machinery/equipment, and project report with projected cash flows.
5. Passport-sized photographs of applicant/partners.

Application Process:
1. Prepare business plan, quotation for items to purchase, and financial/KYC documents.
2. Approach any participating Public/Private Sector Bank, Regional Rural Bank, or NBFC branch.
3. Alternatively, apply online via JanSamarth portal (https://www.jansamarth.in/) under Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana.
4. Fill application form, attach documents, and submit for credit assessment.

Verification Note:
Interest rates vary by lender bank based on RBI/Mudra guidelines. Check details at https://www.mudra.org.in/ or official bank branches.

Scheme Duration, Validity & Last Date:
- Last Date / Application Deadline: There is NO last date or closing deadline for Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY). It is an ongoing central micro-finance scheme.
- Application Availability: Micro-entrepreneurs and small business owners can apply for Mudra loans year-round through commercial banks, RRBs, MFIs, and JanSamarth portal.
