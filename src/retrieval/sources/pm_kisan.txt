PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN)
Official Portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in/
Source Title: PM-Kisan Portal - Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Overview:
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme that provides income support to all landholding farmers' families in the country to enable them to take care of expenses related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs.

Benefits:
- Under the scheme, financial benefit of Rs. 6,000/- per year is provided to all landholding farmer families.
- The benefit is transferred in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000/- each, every four months.
- The amount is transferred directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode.

Eligibility Criteria:
- All landholding farmers' families who have cultivable landholding in their names are eligible.
- A landholding farmer's family is defined as a family consisting of husband, wife, and minor children who own cultivable land in their names as per the land records of the respective State/UT.

Exclusions (Who is NOT Eligible):
The following categories of beneficiaries of higher economic status shall NOT be eligible for benefit under the scheme:
1. Institutional land holders.
2. Farmer families in which one or more of its members belong to the following categories:
   - Former and present holders of constitutional posts.
   - Former and present Ministers/ State Ministers and former/present Members of Lok Sabha/ Rajya Sabha/ State Legislative Assemblies/ State Legislative Councils, former and present Mayors of Municipal Corporations, former and present Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
   - Any serving or retired officers and employees of Central/ State Government Ministries /Offices/Departments and its field units/Central or State PSEs and Attached offices /Autonomous Institutions under Government as well as regular employees of the Local Bodies (Excluding Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV/Group D employees).
   - All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs.10,000/- or more (Excluding Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV/Group D employees).
   - All Persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
   - Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with Professional bodies and carrying out profession by undertaking practice.

Documents Required:
To register and avail PM-KISAN benefits, farmers need to submit the following documents:
1. Aadhaar Card (mandatory for identity verification and DBT transfer).
2. Land Records / Khasra-Khatauni (proof of landholding in the farmer's name as per state land records).
3. Bank Account Details: Bank passbook or cancelled cheque showing account number linked to Aadhaar.
4. Mobile Number linked to Aadhaar for OTP-based verification.
5. Self-Declaration Form confirming eligibility and that the farmer does not fall under the exclusion categories.
Note: Documents may vary slightly by state. Check with your local Patwari/Lekhpal or Common Service Centre (CSC) for state-specific requirements.

How to Apply (Application Process):
Farmers can register for PM-KISAN through any of the following methods:

Method 1 - Online Registration via Official Portal:
- Visit https://pmkisan.gov.in/
- Click on "Farmers Corner" and then "New Farmer Registration".
- Enter Aadhaar number, state, and captcha, then submit.
- Fill in personal, bank, and land details.
- Submit the form and note the registration reference number.

Method 2 - Common Service Centre (CSC):
- Visit your nearest CSC/Jan Seva Kendra with the required documents.
- The CSC operator will register you on the PM-KISAN portal.

Method 3 - Through Patwari / Agriculture Department:
- Contact your local Patwari, Lekhpal, or District Agriculture Officer.
- They can assist in verifying land records and registering on the portal.

Method 4 - Kisaan e-Mitra Chatbot / PM-KISAN Mobile App:
- Use the official PM-KISAN mobile app (available on Google Play Store) or the Kisaan e-Mitra chatbot on the portal to check status and register.

Status Check:
- Farmers can check beneficiary status at https://pmkisan.gov.in/ under "Farmers Corner" → "Beneficiary Status".
- Enter Aadhaar number, account number, or mobile number to check payment status.

Scheme Duration, Validity & Last Date:
- Last Date / Application Deadline: There is NO last date or closing deadline for PM Kisan Samman Nidhi. It is an ongoing Central Sector scheme of the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.
- Continuous Registration: Eligible landholding farmers can apply and register on the PM Kisan portal at any time throughout the year.
- Installment Cycle: Installments of ₹2,000 are released every four months (three times a year) directly into registered bank accounts.
